papertradea fair-launched synth-perp venue on hyperliquid

no seed lp. no pre-mint. no team or vc paper.

the lp starts at $0 and bootstraps from trading. you trade against the pool: winners get paid, or queued first-in, first-out if the lp is short, and losses mint paper, the only claim on future lp revenue.

one way to mint
you can't buy into the lp. paper mints only when a position closes at a loss or is liquidated.
the rate follows the lp
while tracked lp is under ~$2m, every $1 of loss basis mints 100 paper. then the curve decays.
paper earns usdc
stake paper for a share of lp revenue. once the lp clears ~$5m, the excess sweeps to stakers.
